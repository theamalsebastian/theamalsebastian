# 💫 About Me:
I'm Amal, a CSE grad from Kerala who got tired of tutorials that stop at "hello world" and started building things that actually have to survive contact with real users, real data, and real memory limits.<br><br>Some of what that's looked like:<br>- Turned a Streamlit prototype into a full RAG-powered job search engine — sub-1.5s latency, ~87% memory reduction, and a very personal grudge against cross-encoder rerankers eating free-tier RAM<br>- Taught a model to read sign language in real time, then spent more time optimizing FPS and fixing a Keras-to-TFLite conversion than actually training it<br>- Built a multi-tool research agent because I wanted to see how far LangGraph could go before it started arguing with itself<br><br>I like the unglamorous parts of engineering: debugging DNS failures, chasing down why staging works but prod doesn't, and shipping things that are boring in the best way — because boring means they work.<br><br>📫 Always up for talking AI systems, agents, or why your deployment just silently died.<br>
